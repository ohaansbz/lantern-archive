Lantern Archive

History's forgotten stories, brought to light

Lantern Archive is a YouTube channel of short history documentaries: the strange, tragic and overlooked events that rarely make it into the textbooks.

Every episode is built from named sources, and those sources are listed under each video. No invented quotes, no dramatized dialogue, no legends repeated as fact. Where the record is uncertain, the video says so.

Lantern Archive Uploader

Lantern Archive Uploader is the tool the channel's owner uses to publish episodes to the Lantern Archive channel and to read that channel's own statistics and comments. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
